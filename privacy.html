Privacy Policy

Ledgerline and the Ledgerline Ops tool · Last updated 17 November 2026

What Ledgerline Ops is

Ledgerline Ops is a private, internal tool used only by the owner of the Ledgerline YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for permission to:

  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the Ledgerline channel and manage those uploads;
  • read the Ledgerline channel's own information and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers and estimated revenue).

It does not access any other YouTube channel, any other Google service, or any viewer's personal data.

How the data is used and stored

  • Analytics are used only to decide what the channel publishes next and to track progress. They are stored in a private database controlled by the channel owner.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer and, for scheduled runs, as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository.
  • No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Ledgerline Ops uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file and the database removes all stored data.

Viewers

If you watch Ledgerline on YouTube, your data is handled by YouTube under Google's privacy policy. Ledgerline only sees the aggregated analytics YouTube provides to every creator.
